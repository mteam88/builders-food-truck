## Design Context

### Users
Big Buck's owner and teammates monitor the Food Truck Wars bot during the Night Shift challenge, often while the bot runs in a separate VS Code terminal. They need a fast answer to whether it is working, what it ordered, when the phase ends, and how the prior day performed.

### Brand Personality
Focused, candid, and quick to scan: an overnight dispatch board.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, compact control board with restrained warm accents and clear numbers. Avoid glowing cyber styling and decorative dashboard cards.

### Design Principles
- Lead with connection health, phase, and the current order.
- Keep forecasts separate from actual results.
- Mark missing or stale data plainly.
- Explain decisions with food cost, sell-through, profit, and rival activity.
- Keep essential information visible on desktop and phone.
