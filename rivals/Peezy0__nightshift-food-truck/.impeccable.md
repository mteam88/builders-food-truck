## Design Context

### Users
Big Buck's owner and teammates monitor a food truck bot during the Night Shift challenge, often with the live bot running in a separate VS Code terminal. They need to see whether it is connected, what order it sent, when the next phase ends, and how the previous day performed without reading JSON or terminal output.

### Brand Personality
Focused, candid, and quick to scan. The dashboard should feel like a compact dispatch board used during an overnight competition.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, compact control board, as requested by the owner. Use warm food service accents sparingly, clear tabular numbers, and dense but legible sections. Avoid glowing cyber styling and decorative dashboard cards.

### Design Principles
- Put connection health, phase, and the current order first.
- Separate forecast from the previous day's actual outcome.
- Label missing or stale data plainly; never imply the bot is running based only on an old file.
- Show the numbers behind decisions, including food cost, sell-through, profit, and rival activity.
- Work on a phone and desktop without hiding essential details.
